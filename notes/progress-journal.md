## Senior Retrospective Prompts

- **Mental Model Shift**: What fundamental assumption or concept changed for you today?
- **Trade-Off Evaluation**: What alternative approaches did you discard, and what were the decisive trade-offs?
- **Resilience & Failure Modes**: How did you protect this implementation against race conditions, bad inputs, or network drops?
- **Competency Progression**: Which skill in `notes/skill-matrix.md` did this session reinforce or level up?
